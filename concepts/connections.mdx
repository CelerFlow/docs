A connection is an authorization grant that lets your workflows act on behalf of your team in an external app — Gmail, Notion, Slack, HubSpot, Linear, Attio, and more. Connections are shared: connect once from the **Apps** page, and every team member's workflows can use it.

## Where connections live

Open **Apps** from the sidebar (`/{slug}/apps`) to see every connectable app, its status, and a connect / disconnect button. The older `Settings → Connections` path still works — it redirects to **Apps**.

## Two connection sources

All connections are stored in one table, `provider_connections`, with a `source` field that routes everything:

| Source | What it is | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `composio` | OAuth grants brokered by [Composio](https://composio.dev) | Gmail, Notion, Slack, HubSpot, Linear, Airtable, GitHub, Zendesk, Calendly, Zoom, LinkedIn, Google Workspace |
| `first_party_mcp` | A direct OAuth + MCP integration CelerFlow runs itself | Attio |

Disconnecting deletes the row — there's no revoked-but-remembered state. You can always reconnect from a clean slate. Connection membership is team-level, so you don't need to reconnect when a colleague joins.

## Connecting an app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Apps">
    Select **Apps** from the sidebar. See [Settings](/dashboard/settings) for the surrounding navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Click **Connect** next to the app you want to add. CelerFlow starts the OAuth flow for that source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete OAuth">
    A popup opens the provider's consent screen. Authorize access and the popup closes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for confirmation">
    The card polls for status until the account reports as active, then flips to **Connected** — usually within a few seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Stale and deleted connections

If a provider reports that an account no longer exists (a confirmed `404`), the card collapses to a `missing` / `not_connected` state and you'll need to reconnect.

<Warning>
  Transient errors (timeouts, 5xx) do **not** silently disconnect an app. The card keeps treating it as valid. Only a confirmed not-found triggers the missing state.
</Warning>

## Platform-managed capabilities

Some capabilities don't require a connection at all. Web search and page extraction (Firecrawl, Tavily) are **platform-managed** — CelerFlow owns the credentials and billing, so the Planner can always use them without any team setup. See [Integrations — Firecrawl](/integration/firecrawl).

## Supported integrations

Apps the Planner can use today (the connectable set grows over time):

- **Communication & delivery** — [Gmail](/integration/gmail), [Slack](/integration/slack)
- **Docs & knowledge** — [Notion](/integration/notion), Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Drive, Airtable
- **CRM & sales** — [HubSpot](/integration/hubspot), [Attio](/integration/attio)
- **Dev & project** — Linear, GitHub
- **Scheduling & support** — Google Calendar, Calendly, Zoom, Zendesk
- **Web** — [Firecrawl](/integration/firecrawl) (platform-managed)

The [Planner](/concepts/planner) surfaces a connection card in chat if a workflow step needs an app that isn't connected yet — you can connect it in place without leaving the conversation.
