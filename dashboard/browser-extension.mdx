The CelerFlow browser extension powers **browser handoff** — the mechanism that lets a [BioMed](/concepts/biomed) workflow obtain a PDF that sits behind a paywall or institutional login, without CelerFlow ever bypassing the publisher's access controls.

## Why it exists

When a BioMed run reaches a paper whose PDF isn't openly available, the `institutional_access_agent` creates a handoff session and pauses the run. Rather than scraping around the paywall, CelerFlow asks *you* to fetch the file in a browser where you're already authenticated — through your library proxy or institutional login. The capture happens on your authority, in your session.

## How a handoff works

<Steps>
  <Step title="A run pauses">
    A BioMed workflow hits a paywalled paper and opens a handoff session. The workflow waits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You capture the PDF">
    With the extension installed, open the paper in your authenticated browser. The extension captures the PDF and sends it back to the workflow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The run resumes">
    CelerFlow attaches the captured PDF and the workflow continues automatically — on to triage, citation resolution, and Zotero export.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Install and configure

Open the **Browser extension** settings page (`/{slug}/settings/extension`) for the install link and setup steps. Once installed, the extension links to your workspace so handoff sessions route to the right account.

## Availability

The browser handoff extension is available on **all plans**, including Free. The BioMed agents that create handoff sessions, however, require a paid plan — see [Billing](/platform/billing).
