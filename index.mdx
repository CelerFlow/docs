## Say it once. It's done.

Tell CelerFlow what you need in plain English. The Planner reads your description, assembles a step-by-step workflow from a pool of cloud agents, and waits for your confirmation. Once you approve, CelerFlow runs every step automatically — no code, no config, no drag-and-drop.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and create a workspace">
    Go to [celerflow.ai](https://celerflow.ai), sign in, and complete the onboarding flow to set up your team workspace.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect an app">
    Head to **Apps** and link your first tool. Gmail is the fastest place to start.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Describe what you need">
    Click **New chat** and type what you want to happen in plain English. Be specific about the outcome — CelerFlow handles the rest.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm and deploy">
    Review the draft workflow the Planner generates on the canvas. Confirm it and watch each step run in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
