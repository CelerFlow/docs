## What this enables

Firecrawl is the web-search and page-extraction backend for the **Web Search Agent** and the **Scraper Agent**. Use it to scout the web for relevant URLs and fetch full page content.

## No connection required

Firecrawl is **platform-managed** — CelerFlow owns the credentials and billing. There's nothing to connect and no card on the **Apps** page; the Planner can use web search and extraction in any workspace out of the box. The same applies to Tavily, an additional platform-managed search provider.

See [Connections — platform-managed capabilities](/concepts/connections#platform-managed-capabilities).

## Known limitations

Search and fetch are the supported operations. Rate limits and throughput are managed by the platform. For social-timeline content, the Planner routes to the appropriate collector rather than scraping profile URLs directly.
