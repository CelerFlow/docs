<Info>
  **Prerequisites**: a CelerFlow account at [celerflow.ai](https://celerflow.ai) and a Gmail account you're willing to OAuth. Nothing to install locally.
</Info>

## Build your first workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and create a workspace">
    Go to [celerflow.ai](https://celerflow.ai), sign in, and complete onboarding. Pick a use-case and give the workspace a name. Your default platform agents are seeded into the team agent pool automatically.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect Gmail">
    Open **Apps** from the sidebar and click **Connect Gmail**. A popup runs Google OAuth. The card flips to **Connected** once the connection is confirmed — usually within a few seconds.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start a new chat">
    Click **New chat** in the sidebar. Describe the outcome in one or two sentences. For example:

    > Every morning, search for the latest AI policy news, summarize the top stories, and email me the digest.

    Be specific about what to do and where the result should land.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Answer the Planner's follow-ups">
    The Interview agent may ask a clarifying question, then produce a **Requirement Brief** that summarizes your goal. Confirm the brief and the Planner assembles a draft workflow. If a required app isn't connected, a connection card appears in chat — connect it in place. See [Approvals](/concepts/approvals) for what each pause means.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm and deploy">
    Review the draft DAG on the canvas. When it looks right, the Planner asks **"Ready to deploy?"**. Confirm it and the workflow goes live. Redeploying later upgrades the same workflow in place — no duplicates.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Watch it run">
    Open the workflow from the sidebar to see run history, step-level logs, and the result. Updates stream in real time over Supabase Realtime. See [Executions](/dashboard/executions) for the full detail view.
  </Step>
</Steps>
